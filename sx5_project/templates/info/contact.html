How the points work

The rules

  • After each race you get points on three lists: everyone, your gender, and your age category.
  • Your points are your finishing place. First place gets 1 point, second place gets 2 points, and so on.
  • On each list, the runner with the fewest points at the end of the series wins.
  • You need to run at least 5 of the 6 races to be in the final standings.
  • If you run all 6 races, your worst result is left out and your best 5 count.

During the series

  • Open a race and choose "Series standings" to see the standings after that race.
  • If you have run every race so far, your worst result is already left out.
  • A runner who has missed too many races to reach 5 is taken out of the standings, and everyone behind them moves up one place. Say someone beat you at King's Park, then missed Linn Park and Rouken Glen. They are taken out, and you move up.
  • Select a runner in the standings to see how their points add up.

Example 1: Alex runs all 6 races

  • King's Park: 3rd overall.
  • Linn Park: 5th overall.
  • Rouken Glen: 4th overall.
  • Pollok Park: 2nd overall.
  • Bellahouston Park: 6th overall.
  • Queen's Park: 1st overall.

Alex's worst result, 6th at Bellahouston Park, is left out. The other five add up: 3 + 5 + 4 + 2 + 1 = 15 points.

Example 2: Alex misses one race

  • King's Park: 2nd overall.
  • Linn Park: did not run.
  • Rouken Glen: 3rd overall.
  • Pollok Park: 4th overall.
  • Bellahouston Park: 1st overall.
  • Queen's Park: 5th overall.

Alex ran 5 races, so all 5 count and nothing is left out: 2 + 3 + 4 + 1 + 5 = 15 points.

Reading the results

Positions and points

General
Your place among everyone in the race.
Gender
Your place among male, female or non-binary runners.
Category
Your place in your age category.
DNF
Did not finish. A DNF is scored as a 2-hour finish.
